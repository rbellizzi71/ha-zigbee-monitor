# Zigbee Monitor

Watches your Zigbee2MQTT devices and alerts you when one goes offline or disappears.

- Choose which devices to watch from a panel in the Home Assistant sidebar.
- See at a glance which devices are online, offline or missing, with a history of every change.
- Get notified on your phone, without repeated alerts or false alarms when Zigbee2MQTT restarts,
  and choose which kinds of alerts you get.
- Find out why the network fails: automatic incident reports and traffic per device.
- Use the sensors it creates in your own automations and dashboards.

Requires Zigbee2MQTT with availability enabled. See the Documentation tab for details.
